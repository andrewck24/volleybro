apple-splash-dynamic

Proposal · refactor · capability apple-splash

Why

The nine Apple PWA splash images are hand-exported PNGs from April 2024 whose background no longer matches the app's current --primary color. Every rebrand or token change forces re-exporting all nine binaries by hand. A single parametric generator removes the stored assets and lets the splash track the design tokens.

What changes

  • Add a dynamic route that renders the launch screen with next/og ImageResponse: a bare reconstructed “V” mark (white + coral SVG polygons from the icon geometry) centered on a solid --primary background. Width/height come from the route segment, so one handler serves every device size.
  • Repoint each appleWebApp.startupImage entry to the route (carrying the exact device pixel size). The per-device media queries are unchanged — Apple requires them and offers no wildcard.
  • Extend coverage from nine iPhone-only configs to fifteen — iPhone through the iPhone 17 generation (incl. iPhone Air) plus common iPad sizes. Because screens are generated, added coverage is just new device entries, no new assets.
  • Remove the nine static PNGs under the public apple-splash directory.
  • Out of scope: Android/Chrome splash (manifest-based) — handled by the sibling android-splash-manifest change.
