---
title: Roles
description: The ADMIN / MEMBER hierarchy, the team-designated owner, and who may manage whom.
---

A member's role ranks ADMIN above MEMBER. The owner is not a role: the team names it in `ownerPlayerId`, and the owner is always an ADMIN. Only a member holds a role — an invitee holds the role their invitation offered, but it has no effect until they join, and an unlinked player has no role at all.

## Current behavior

- `hasTeamRole` in `src/entities/player.ts` compares a member's role against a required rank; `canManageTeam` is `hasTeamRole` at ADMIN or above.
- Only the owner and admins may rename a team or change its nickname; the request may carry no other field.
- The owner and other admins may change or delete an admin, a regular member, an invitee or an unlinked player. A regular member may manage no one.
- The owner is nobody's target for a role change or deletion. Ownership moves only through transfer: one conditional write on the team that succeeds only while the caller is still the owner, so a concurrent transfer loses with `NOT_TEAM_OWNER`. Only a joined ADMIN may receive ownership, and the previous owner stays an ADMIN. Transferring ownership to the current owner is refused.
- Nobody may target their own player for a role change or deletion; leaving the team is the path for that instead, and it keeps the roster row as an unlinked player rather than removing it.
- `refuseToManagePlayer` in `src/entities/player.ts` is the single function both `update-role` and `remove-player` use to decide whether the caller may act on a given target, so the two write paths cannot drift from each other.

## Constraints

- A write path that changes or removes a player must check the target through `refuseToManagePlayer`, not only the caller's own role.
- The owner is read from the team, never inferred from a player role; a role change or deletion must never be able to demote or remove the owner.

## Known gaps

- `update-role`, `remove-player` and a few other use cases (find-game, cancel-invitation, update-player-info) read their target before authorizing against it.
- `PATCH /api/teams/{teamId}` and its lineups route authorize inside the route handler rather than the use case, unlike the pattern the rest of team management follows.
- The transfer write is conditional on the owner only: a target demoted between the read and the write becomes the owner holding the MEMBER role.
- Ownership-transfer acceptance — requiring the recipient to accept before the role actually moves, the way several reference products do — is not built yet.
