The design system is the layer the App and Landing surfaces share for appearance and interaction.

## Current behavior

- Color, elevation and depth tokens, and the rules for the app's background layers, are documented in root `DESIGN.md`.
- Blueprint presents the authoritative document by section alongside live token and component samples. `.impeccable/design.json` is generated from that document; it is not separately edited.
- Component layers, list-row composition, and loading, progress and empty states are described in the Components sub-capability.
- Dialog ownership, dialog controls, and how a dialog closes are described in the Overlays sub-capability.

## Known gaps

- Adopted icon-button and chip sizing rules are documented in `DESIGN.md`; their implementation belongs to the next button-size delivery shard.
