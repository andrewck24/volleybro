title: Forms
description: Where a submit button lives, how it shows progress, and the rules that keep validation ahead of any close or navigation.
---

Forms use React Hook Form through `Form` in `src/components/ui/form.tsx`, and `Button` from `src/components/ui/button.tsx`.

## Current behavior

- A submit button sits inside the form boundary it submits and is `type="submit"`. In a dialog that place is `DialogFooter`, with the form wrapping `DialogHeader`, `DialogBody` and `DialogFooter`. A validation failure therefore never reaches the success path: neither the browser nor RHF calls `onSubmit`, so nothing has to remember not to close.
- A form shared by an `@modal` dialog and a `(workspace)` full page renders its own `DialogBody` and `DialogFooter` in both; on a full page they are plain padded containers.
- An asynchronous submit button shows its progress with `loading` and `loadingText`. `loadingText` replaces the whole content, icon included, with a spinner followed by the text, so the spinner always leads.
- Icon position carries meaning: an icon on the left says what a button does (save, add, confirm); an icon on the right says where it takes you (an arrow).
- A failed request shows its error, as a toast or a root error, and does not report success; the entered values stay.
- The team and player forms keep an unsaved draft in `sessionStorage` through `useFormDraft` (`src/hooks/use-form-draft.ts`), one per form and record, written only once the form is dirty. Reopening the form merges the draft over its defaults, and a draft that cannot be read is ignored. A successful submit or a confirmed discard clears it; otherwise it lasts as long as the browser tab's session.
- Expanding a dirty dialog into its full page suppresses the browser's leave warning once, because the draft carries over to the full page.

## Constraints

- `Button` has no default `type`, so any `Button` inside a form boundary that does not submit must declare `type="button"`, or it submits the form. `DialogHeader`'s own controls already do.
- A loading button whose children include an icon must pass `loadingText`, or the spinner and the icon show together. Types cannot catch this.
- A `<form>` must never be nested inside another; the browser drops the inner one.

## Known gaps

- The player edit page keeps its save button inside the basic-information section rather than in `DialogFooter`. The membership section's invite flow has its own `<form>`, and a boundary wrapping the footer would nest it. Moving membership management to the player's page, with its own dialogs, removes the exception.
- `min-h-0 flex-1` is repeated on the four forms that wrap a dialog, so a change to `DialogContent`'s layout must update each; ADR-0038 explains why there is no shared wrapper.
- The `type="button"` rule relies on people remembering it. Defaulting `Button` to `type="button"` would change `team/form.tsx`, which relies on the implicit submit.
- `Button`'s `RiLoader4Line` and the `Spinner` in `src/components/ui/spinner.tsx` are two spinners in use.
