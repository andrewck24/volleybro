## Current behavior

- The page is `src/app/page.tsx`: it renders `StatusBarColor` with `--free-zone` and the `Landing` component from `src/components/landing/index.tsx`, and its `viewport.themeColor` supplies the light and dark teal for first paint.
- One `RallyProvider` wraps hero, walkthrough and stats. The hero court and the stats carousel are its only clock consumers; the walkthrough between them is passed in server-rendered and never re-renders on a beat.
- Every call-to-action button (header, hero, closing) is a `CTAButton` (`src/components/landing/cta-button.tsx`) labelled 開始記錄.
- Page copy lives in `src/components/landing/copy.ts`, in Traditional Chinese.
- The kit list renders seven rows from `KIT` in `copy.ts`; the two planned capabilities carry the 開發中 badge (`dev-badge.tsx`) and are otherwise styled like the rest.
- The footer carries the theme switcher (see Visual design), GitHub and feedback links, and the author credit.

## Known gaps

- There is no end-to-end lane for the landing; behavior is held by component tests under `src/components/landing/__tests__/` and by manual browser checks.
- The kit list's heading alignment uses `text-box` trim and a measured 0.17em offset for CJK ink; this is unverified on WebKit.
