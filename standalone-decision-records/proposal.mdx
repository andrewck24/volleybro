<TLDR>
A decision record stops belonging to a Change. All records move to one flat `blueprint/content/decisions/` directory, numbered at birth, with no per-capability copies and no promotion step; Feature pages render them by filtering on `capabilities`, the field that used to be called `targets`. The schema drops to five required fields, deletes `originDecision`, and moves to version 2, while version 1 records published on the store branch keep rendering through the compatibility layer. The Fix path may correct a record, but not its `decision` body or its `supersededBy`.
</TLDR>

## Why now

Three facts, all measured against the tree as it stands:

1. `blueprint/content/features/**/decisions/` holds **65 files containing 45 distinct records**. Nineteen numbers occur more than once; `D29` occurs three times.
2. For **all 45** records, the set of directories the record sits in is **exactly** the set of IDs inside its own `targets` array. Zero mismatches. The directory tree carries no information the file does not already carry.
3. Every copy of every duplicated record is byte-identical today apart from its `$schema` relative path — so the copies have not drifted **yet**, and deduplicating them is mechanical rather than a merge.

The second fact is the argument. A duplicate that restates a field is not redundancy that needs a consistency check; it is redundancy that needs deleting.

## Change boundaries

- Move all 45 distinct records to `blueprint/content/decisions/D<n>-<slug>.json`, deleting the 20 duplicate files and fixing each record's `$schema` relative path.
- Rename `targets` to `capabilities` in the schema, the hand-written validator, `DecisionTimeline`, and every record.
- Move the schema to version 2: five required fields, `originDecision` deleted, `originChange` kept and optional.
- Replace the relative-path imports on around a dozen Feature `index.mdx` pages with a capability-filtered render backed by a build-time index.
- Extend the legacy compatibility layer to upconvert version 1 records so the 48 records published inside store-branch Change pages keep rendering.
- Rewrite the Decision-record contract in `WORKFLOW.md` and the ADR sections of `docs/agents/domain.md` to the new model, and redraw the Fix path line at the `decision` and `supersededBy` fields.
- **Out of scope** — `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s code-comment citation rule still distinguishes a Feature number from a Change's own `D2`, a distinction this Change removes. That file belongs to `reviewer-standards-split`, which is sequenced after this Change and carries the rewrite.
- **Out of scope** — the records already published inside Change pages on the `blueprint-changes` store branch. They are history and are not migrated.
- **Out of scope** — ATE-167's remaining two items (schema validation in CI, store-branch retention policy). Its duplicate-consistency item is deleted by this Change rather than solved by it; the other two are re-scoped once this lands.

## Bootstrap note

These six records are born under the model they replace: inside a Change's `proposal/decisions/`, in version 1, with `targets` and all nine fields filled. A rule that frees decision records from the delivery lifecycle cannot be recorded by a record that is already free of it. They move to `blueprint/content/decisions/` in version 2 form during this Change's own Apply, as part of the same migration pass as the other 45. This is what a bootstrap looks like, not a defect in the model.
